EdTech Lab | Center for Education Technology (CET) | IIT Jodhpur
The EdTech Lab , located in Room 225 Annex of the New Faculty Office Building , is designed to offer a world-class, hands-on learning environment in e-learning design and educational technology development . Tailored especially for ITEP and PhD students , the lab is equipped with a smart interactive panel, high-performance computers, pen tablets, essential software, providing a comprehensive setup for cutting-edge EdTech education.
