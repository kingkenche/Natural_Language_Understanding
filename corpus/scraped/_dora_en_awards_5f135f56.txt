Awards | Office of Resources and Alumni (DORA) | IIT Jodhpur
The Office of Resources & Alumni at the Institute plays a pivotal role in mobilizing financial resources from a diverse range of stakeholders, including industries, philanthropic foundations, individual donors, and the global alumni community. Leveraging the Institute’s registration under the Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) framework, the office is empowered to seek and secure contributions from corporate entities and charitable organizations to support a wide spectrum of academic, research, infrastructural, and developmental initiatives.
In its mission to foster meaningful partnerships, the Office of Resources & Alumni curates and facilitates a variety of strategic programs and engagement activities. These initiatives are designed to create dynamic platforms for interaction and collaboration between industry leaders, corporate representatives, faculty members, students, and staff of IIT Jodhpur. Through such engagements, the IIT Jodhpur not only strengthens institutional ties with external stakeholders but also nurtures a culture of innovation and mutual growth.
Furthermore, the Office of Resources & Alumni plays an instrumental role in coordinating and managing scholarship programs generously sponsored by corporations, philanthropists, and well-wishers. These scholarships are vital in supporting meritorious and financially underprivileged students, thereby reinforcing the Institute’s commitment to inclusive and accessible education.
