Assistantship | Master of Science | IIT Jodhpur
Students enrolled in the M.Sc. Programs are not provided any assistantship or scholarship from within the Institute. But, candidates can pursue external funding agencies providing assistantship or scholarship for M.Sc. Here is a list of such agencies:  (1)  INSPIRE Scholarship by DST:  http://www.inspire-dst.gov.in/scholarship.html     (2) Kishore Vaigyanik Protsahan Yojana:  http://kvpy.iisc.ernet.in/main/index.htm
