Doctoral Program | Quantum Information and Computation | IIT Jodhpur
Currently we offer two types of doctoral programs in the QIC domain . One is the Inter-Disciplinary Ph.D in Quantum Information and Communication(IDRP-QIC) program and the other is the Physics Department Ph.D program. Both have different selection criteria. We are in the process of establishing several national and international collaborations with leading institutes in this domain. We are always on the lookout for talented and passionate students who are willing to go that extra mile. We also require post doctoral researchers with similar drive and focus. Students who have already obtained their PhD or are about to finish their PhD are encouraged to apply for postdoctoral positions in our group. Students interested in short term projects are also encouraged to directly mail to the respective faculties.
