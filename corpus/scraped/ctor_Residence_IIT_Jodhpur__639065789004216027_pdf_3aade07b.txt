Scope of Work:
The scope of work for providing and fixing a conical-shaped tensile fabric shed at the Director’s
Residence, IIT Jodhpur, covering an approximate area of 4180sq.ft. with a canopy pillar height of about
12 ft, shall include the following:
1) Supply, fabrication, and installation of conical-shaped tensile fabric  sheds with mild steel
(MS) structural framework, including powder coating / approved paint finish.
2)  Providing and fixing 900 GSM PVDF-coated tensile fabric, coated on both sides, suitable
for outdoor use.
3)  Fabrication of the structural system using MS pipes/tubes/rolled sections, including base
plates, anchor bolts, and all necessary welding works.
4) Application of anti-corrosive paints and finishing coats / powder coating to all exposed
5) Complete installation at site, including all labour, materials, tools, equipment, lifting, and
positioning of the shed structure.
