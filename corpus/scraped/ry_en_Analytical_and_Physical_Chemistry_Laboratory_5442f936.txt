Analytical and Physical Chemistry Laboratory
This lab is newly set up in the academic year 2018-2019, the lab procured equipment such as UV-visible spectrometers, FTIR Spectrometers, Fluorescence Spectrometer, Electrochemical Workstation, Contact Angle Meter, Polarimeter for carrying out state of the experiments in Analytical and Physical Chemistry. This laboratory can accommodate 40 students together for carrying out group experiments.
