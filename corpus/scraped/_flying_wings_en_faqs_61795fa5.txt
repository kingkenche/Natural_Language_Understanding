FAQs | Flying Wings : National Level Hackathon 2026 | IIT Jodhpur
Who is eligible to participate?
The competition is open to both students and industry professionals/startups, though they compete in separate tracks.
What are the registration fees for the first round?
Round 1 fees are ₹7,000 for student teams and ₹10,000 for professionals.
What are the registration fees for the second round?
The ₹20,000 fee covers the competition entry and food/accommodation for 3 members. Extra members can be added at ₹6,000 each.
Can startups showcase their proprietary technology?
Yes! Exhibition stalls are available for startups to showcase products and pilots, subject to approval and sponsorship tier.
What are the specific technical challenge areas? +
The three verticals are:
Cardboard Drone Design
Drones in Electronic Warfare (EW)
CCCD @ IITJ
