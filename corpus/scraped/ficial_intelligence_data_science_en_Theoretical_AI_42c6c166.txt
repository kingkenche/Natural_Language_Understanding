Theoretical AI | School of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science | IIT Jodhpur
Theoretical AI explores the foundational principles and mathematical frameworks that drive artificial intelligence, focusing on algorithms, models, and theories behind intelligent systems. It seeks to advance understanding of learning, reasoning, and decision-making processes in machines. By building a strong theoretical foundation, this field pushes the boundaries of AI innovation and its applications in real-world scenarios.
