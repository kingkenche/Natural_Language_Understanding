Capacity Building Program on World Water Day (22 March 2023)
A training and capacity-building program has been conducted on "Sustainability of Water Resources" on  March 22, 2023 , in commemoration of  World Water Day . The participants in the program were officials from state and union territory Public Health Engineering Departments (PHEDs).  Dr. Vikky Anand  delivered a lecture on " Sustainability of Water Sources " and presented several case studies of different organizations working towards it.  Dr. Kirti Sankhala  presented different technologies for water treatment and discussed the participatory approaches for sustainable water management.
