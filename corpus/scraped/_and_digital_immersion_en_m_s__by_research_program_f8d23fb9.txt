M.S. by Research Program
The Centre of Excellence for Arts and Digital Immersion (ADI) at IIT Jodhpur introduces the M.S. (by Research) program, a groundbreaking initiative merging traditional and emerging art forms with advanced technologies. Launched in July 2023, this flagship program fosters innovation, interdisciplinary research, and sustainable artistic practices. By blending creative arts with cutting-edge tools, it offers students a unique platform to redefine artistic expression and shape the future of art in the digital age. Graduate Attributes Employ AI tools in creative arts. Work with mixed-media art applications. Integrate VR, AR, MR, and XR technologies into visual and performing arts. Design gaming and art-based applications. Learning Outcomes Master tools for AI-driven creativity. Analyze mixed-media art challenges and opportunities. Apply emerging tech trends to artistic practices. Innovate in digital gaming and creative outputs. Program Structure 1.) Eligibility: Bachelor's (B.Tech. or equivalent, 4 years) or Master's (M.A./M.Sc., 5 years) with a strong academic record. 2.) Duration: Full-time: 2–4 years. Part-time: 3–5 years. 3.) Credits: Coursework: 24 credits. Thesis: 36 credits. 4.) Course Flexibility: Students customize coursework from advanced courses across disciplines, guided by the ADI Research Committee. Research Areas 1.) AI and Creative Arts: Utilize AI for generating artwork, composing music, storytelling, and exploring ethical questions around authorship and creativity. Push the boundaries of artistic expression with AI-assisted tools. 2.) Mixed-Media Art: Redefine art with immersive technologies like VR, AR, and MR. Explore themes like identity, AI, data privacy, and social media through interactive installations and virtual environments. Program Features Opportunities for collaborative projects across disciplines. Practice-based outputs, including new art forms and entrepreneurial ventures. Focus on inclusivity and livelihood generation for disenfranchised artisans. Flexible coursework and simultaneous research options tailored to student interests.
