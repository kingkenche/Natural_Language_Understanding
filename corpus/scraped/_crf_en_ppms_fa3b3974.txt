ETO measurement can provide the resistance and resistivity (if geometry is known) of materials over a wide range of temperatures and magnetic fields, allowing for the study of phenomena like magnetoresistance and the effect of temperature on electrical properties.
VSM measurement can provide the magnetization, magnetic susceptibility, and other magnetic properties of bulk, nanoparticles and thin film materials, which is important for understanding magnetic reversal processes, designing new magnetic devices, and characterizing magnetic phenomena in materials
