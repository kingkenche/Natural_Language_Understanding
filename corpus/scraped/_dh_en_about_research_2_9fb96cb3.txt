About Research | Digital Humanities | IIT Jodhpur
Focus Areas DH at IIT Jodhpur is premised on project-oriented knowledge production on the practical application of methods and involves interdisciplinary collaboration workflows. Examples of research emphases include (but are not limited to): Digital Cultures – Past and present Digital Societies Digital Heritage (Preservation, Conservation, Restoration, Recreation) Thematic Computation Reading of Novels
Digital Economy / Internet Economy Multimodal Data Analytics
Digital Epistemologies and Methods
The following are the core overarching research goals Building a digital repository of data and ontology Building computational tools to understand cultural changes (text as a digitally mediated object; contextualizing the digital object; asking questions of it in terms of its material aspects) Critically analyzing digital embodiment.
