1. Celebration of Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas:
The institute organized a series of events to celebrate the JanJatiya Guarav Diwas from 15th to 26th November 2024 , marking the significant contributions and cultural heritage of India's tribal communities. This period marks the birth anniversary of Birsa Munda , a tribal freedom fighter and leader who played a key role in the struggle against British colonial rule. His life and sacrifices are central to the theme of the celebrations, as he remains an iconic figure in the tribal history of India.
The celebration highlights the historical importance, struggles, and achievements of tribal groups in India. Throughout the celebration, the institute hosted cultural performances , including skit performance by the students, " kalbeliya"dances and online competitions for students showcasing the rich diversity of tribal art forms.
A key focus was on educating participants about the legacy of leaders like Birsa Munda , whose contributions to India's freedom struggle were remembered through talks and film screenings.
2. Rashtriya Ekta Diwas celebration on 28th Oct. 2024:
Program Highlights :
Documentary on Sardar Patel
KeyNote speaker Dr. G Satheesh Reddy
The Rashtriya Ekta Diwas (National Unity Day) was celebrated on 28th October 2024 at the institute, honoring the vision and leadership of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel , India’s first Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister, who is often regarded as the 'Iron Man of India'. The day was dedicated to fostering national integration and unity, celebrating the values of harmony and togetherness that Sardar Patel stood for.
As part of the celebrations, the institute organized a special screening of a documentary on Sardar Patel’s life and contributions, which highlighted his pivotal role in the integration of princely states into the Indian Union after independence. The documentary showcased his unwavering commitment to unifying a newly independent India and his efforts in shaping the country's social and political landscape.
In addition to the documentary, Dr. G Sateesh Reddy delivered speech on the occasion & various cultural activities were held to engage the students and faculty.
The celebration was a reminder of the values of national integration, strength in diversity , and solidarity that Sardar Patel instilled in the country, encouraging everyone to come together to build a prosperous and harmonious India.
3. International Mother Language Day Celebration:
On the occasion of International Mother Language Day celebrated at the institute on 21st February 2024, Padma Shri Sheen Nizam Kaif (Shri Shiv Kishan Bissa) , a renowned linguist and cultural advocate, highlighted the profound significance of mother languages in shaping personal identity, culture, and society. His address emphasized the essential role of language as a tool for communication, preserving traditions, and fostering a sense of belonging.
Shri Shiv Kishan Bissa spoke about the deep connection between language and cultural heritage, stressing that every language is a repository of a community’s history, values, and collective wisdom. He underscored how the loss of a language equates to the loss of a unique worldview and cultural expression, urging everyone to make conscious efforts to preserve and promote their mother languages.
4. International Women's Day on 8th March 2025:
The institute celebrated International Women's Day on 8th March 2025 , a day dedicated to honoring the achievements, contributions, and resilience of women across the world. The theme for this year’s celebration was " Empower, Inspire, Achieve ", reflecting the institute's commitment to promoting gender equality and supporting women in all spheres of life.
The event kicked off with an inspiring keynote address by Dr. Ranjana Desai, Former Director, Ummed Hospital, Jodhpur who shared her personal journey and the importance of empowering women through education, equal opportunities, and support. The speech highlighted the need for continued efforts in breaking down societal barriers, promoting women’s rights, and ensuring gender parity in every field. Further, Ms. Anuradha Avdani, Co-founder of Anubandh- Old Age Home in Jodhpur also shared her thoughts on the occasion.
