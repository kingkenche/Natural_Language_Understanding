Nuclear Magnetic Resonance(NMR)
Nuclear Magnetic Resonance(NMR)
Model: AVANCE III HD, Ascend TM WB, 500 MHz
Frequency: 500 MHz
Magnetic Field: 11.7 Tesla
1. BBFO Probe (Liquid State): Broad Band Fluorine observe
Inner coil optimized for observation of nuclei between 31P and 15N (BBFO: for 19F as well)
Sample diameter: 5 mm (BBFO)
Switch between decoupling nuclei using an automated tuning and matching device (ATM).
Z-Gradient at all frequencies
Variable Temperature range from +150 to -150
Maximum gradient strength is 50 gauss/cm
2. TBI Probe (Liquid State):
A 5mm Inverse Triple-resonance probe-head in multinuclear version,
TBI (1H, 31P, BB) with Z-gradient.
The BB range is 109Ag-31P.
Variable Temperature range from +150 to -150
3. Diffusion Probe: 1H(offers 3D gradients)
Types of Experiment can be conducted: 1D NMR, DEPT, 2D NMR: COSY, TCOSY, DOSY, NOESY, ROESY, HMQC, HSQC, Water Suppression Experiment, Multinuclear NMR, Variable temperature measurement
5 mg for 1H and minimum 10 mg for 13C NMR experiment. Compounds should be highly pure and soluble in 0.6 ml Deuterated solvent.
Materials characterization can be done using NMR Diffusion and relaxation experiments.
Structural elucidation of organic, inorganic, and organometallic compounds; purity testing and compound identification.
Analysis of binding interactions, kinetics, and conformational changes in drug discovery and molecular recognition.
