SUMMER INTERNSHIP HIGHLIGHTS
SUMMER INTERNSHIP HIGHLIGHTS
With  a  glistening  footing  in  the  fast-paced  world  of  corporates  and
business education, the School of Management & Entrepreneurship feels
fortunate  and  yet  more  confident  to  continue  with  its  legacy  of  hitting
the One Hundred Percent Summer Placements mark.
We  feel  privileged  to  express  all  our  ardour  to  the  diversified  and
significant array of corporates who allowed the MBA cohort of 2021-23
to  associate  themselves  with  on-the-job  learning  at  their  upskilled
workplaces.  The  summer  internship  process  was  completed  in  two
phases, with 56 companies showing interest and making 95 offers to the
MBA  students.  To  name  a  few,  BFSI,  IT/Tech,  Manufacturing,
Healthcare,  Operations,  etc.,  were  a  part  of  the  diversified companies'
domain  portfolio.  Following  the  same,  Consulting,  Marketing,  Research,
Branding,  eCommerce,  HR,  Supply  Chain  Analyst,  Social  Media,  Risk
Management,  Retail  Banking,  Wealth  Management  etc.,  were  some  of
the many roles offered to the entire batch.
It  is  an  honour  for  our  institute  to  celebrate  this  day  and  applaud  all
Faculty  and  Students'  efforts,  in  perhaps  one  of  the  toughest  times.
Culminating  with  utmost  determination  in  the  committed  duration,
ensuring 100% Summer Placements yet again has been a benchmark for
this new B-School.
