Computing Facilities | Digital Infrastructure & Automation (DIA) | IIT Jodhpur
The Computer Center provides computing facilities for Faculty, Staff members, and Students to access internal resources, use application software. User may ask for the following available facilities by sending email to support_cc@iitj.ac.in.
Two lab with capacity of 100 and 60 computer terminals running on Windows and GNU/Linux operating systems.
Access to IIT Jodhpur licensed software like Microsoft 365, Matlab, Mathematica, Cadence, Mentor-Graphics, Ansys, PSCAD, Solidworks, and orCad for its academic and research purposes.
High Performance Computing (HPC) cluster for scientific research.
