Registration | Winter School | IIT Jodhpur
Registration fees: 1500 INR per school
Accommodation Charges: 400 per day
Accommodation will be provided on a sharing basis. If you need single accommodation, please write an email to winterschool@cse.iitj.ac.in
Accommodation charges include breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Mattress, bedsheet, pillow, and blanket will be provided Confirmation of the registration will be sent after verification of registration fees.
All the charges are excluding GST.
Hostel charges of a full day will be applicable, even if occupied for a shorter duration.
The hostel accommodation is only for the participants.
Regular IITJ students do not have to pay accommodation fees
No refund will be provided.
