Central Workshop | Mechanical Engineering | IIT Jodhpur
The central workshop is the central facility of Institute, consisting of various workshops such as Welding shop, Carpentry shop, Fitting shop, Sheet metal shop, Foundry and Heat treatment shop and Machine shop. Undergraduate Students get hands-on experience in the above sections by doing the job work and carrying out projects as part of their coursework and also students utilize the facilities for fabrication purpose of their academic projects. It also supports the R&D projects of the institute handled by various faculty members and PhD and M.Tech. thesis work of research scholars by providing them assistance in the fabrication of their research set-ups. The following machines and equipment are available in the Central Workshop: Welding Fume Extraction Down Draft Table, Multi-process Welding Equipment, Portable Single Phase MIG/MAG, AC/DC Welding Equipment, MIG/MAG Welding Equipment, Treadle-operated Shearing Machine, Hand-operated Folding Machine, Kaizen Muffle Furnace, Hand-operated Jeeny or Burying Machine, Motorized Circle Cutting Machine, Hand-operated Circle Cutting Machine, Hydraulic Shearing Machine, Portable Heating Plant, Portable Hardening plant, Forging Heating Plant, Aluminium Melting Plant, Fitting Table, Mould Making Facility, Portable Tool Grinder.
