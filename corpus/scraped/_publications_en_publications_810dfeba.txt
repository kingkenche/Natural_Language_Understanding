Welcome to the Office of Publications
The Office of Publications at the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur is committed to enhancing the institute’s communication and outreach through high-quality publications. Our mission is to disseminate the achievements, research, and activities of IIT Jodhpur to a global audience, fostering a deeper understanding and appreciation of the institute’s contributions. We oversee the production of a variety of publications, including research journals, newsletters, annual reports, and promotional materials. Our team ensures that all content is accurate, engaging, and reflective of the institute’s standards of excellence. The Office of Publications works closely with faculty, researchers, and administrative departments to gather, curate, and present information in a compelling manner. We are dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of editorial and design quality in all our publications. Our office also manages the digital presence of IIT Jodhpur’s publications, ensuring accessibility and broad dissemination through online platforms. We aim to enhance the visibility and impact of the institute’s work by reaching a diverse audience, including academia, industry, and the general public. By promoting IIT Jodhpur’s achievements and initiatives, the Office of Publications plays a crucial role in strengthening the institute’s reputation and influence in the global academic community.
The Institute Publication Committee (IPC) at IIT Jodhpur was constituted first in August 2019 with the task of preparing and publishing various Institute Publications like TechScape Journal, Annual Report of the Institute, and Institute Brochures. Over the years, the Committee has published several issues of TechScape journal and other publications, the softcopy of which can be found at this site.
