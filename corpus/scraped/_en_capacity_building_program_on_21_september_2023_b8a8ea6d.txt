Capacity Building Program on 21 September 2023
Prof. Pradip Kumar Tewari  delivered a lecture on " Water Sustainability " and discussed case studies to better understand the challenges and technical solutions.  Dr. Vikky Anand  delivered a lecture on "Sustainability of Water Sources" and presented several case studies of different organizations working towards it.  Dr. Shobhana Singh  presented “ Smart Water Grid for Sustainability of Water Supply ”, After that  Dr. Saran Adhar  delivered the lecture on “ Advances in Flood & Drought Risk Assessment & Management for a Sustainable Future ”.
