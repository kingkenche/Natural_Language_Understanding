Social Outreach | School of Liberal Arts | IIT Jodhpur
Through its academic and research programmes, the School of Liberal Arts collaborates with a diversity of stakeholders in addressing societal and environmental concerns through high-quality, socially beneficial, foundational, and impactful solutions via an adoption of a transdisciplinary approach. The faculty members of the school have lent their expertise to various National Missions such as Unnat Bharat Abhiyan, Ek Bharat Shrest Bharat, Ishan Vikas, Vigyan Jyoti, Swach Bharat Abhiyan etc.
