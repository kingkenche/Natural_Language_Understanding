With the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment Jodhpur Block, the permaculture based kitchen garden project for its hostels across jodhpur is going on from March 2020.
This program helps in making the government hostels Atmanirbhar under the thought of the nutrition program by the government of India. This project can be considered as an example towards the government schemes such as Poshan Abhiyaan program. It can be considered good for schemes under the Ministry of Agriculture, Ministry of Health, Skill India, Ministry of Education and AYUSH.
For more information of the kitchen garden project you may contact RuTAG (Office of PSA, Delhi) and Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Jodhpur Block.
