IIT Jodhpur Marudhara Foundation, a Section 8 Company with aim to encourage, promote and facilitate education and research and other activities of the Indian Institute of Technology, Jodhpur. It also works to encourage dialogue with the Industries for Research and Consulting projects. Several programs and events are also conducted to promote Interaction among the PAN IIT Alumni members and the Institutefor dacilitating academic, Industrial and enterpreneurial support and Services.
