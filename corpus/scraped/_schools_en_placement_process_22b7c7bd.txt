Placement Process | School of Management & Entrepreneurship | IIT Jodhpur
SME’s Corporate Relations Team consists of both students as well as faculty, having prior work experience in the industry and oversees the recruitment process. The student Placements Committee as part of the Corporate Relations Team facilitate all interactions with industries. Recruiters who wish to conduct presentations and participate in the Summer Internship, Lateral or Final placement process at SME Jodhpur are requested to contact the Placement Committee at placement_sme@iitj.ac.in.
