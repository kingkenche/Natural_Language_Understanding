Raman Spectrometer | Central Research Facility (CRF) | IIT Jodhpur
Model: MULTI WAVE NOMADIC TM
Identifies material "fingerprints" based on vibrational frequencies, offering precise molecular and structural insights.
Equipped with three excitation lasers: 355 nm, 532 nm, and 785 nm.
Measures Raman shift from 50 to 4000 cm⁻¹.
Used for identifying chemical bonds, determining phases (symmetry), analyzing stress/strain (bond length changes), and probing the electronic structure of nanomaterials.
Can analyse thin films, solid pellets, and powdered samples.
Objective lenses of 10X, 20X, 50X, and 100X magnification.
Identifies and verifies active pharmaceutical ingredients and excipients in tablets without destroying the sample.
Detects and identifies unknown substances like drugs and explosives, or traces evidence in forensics quickly and accurately.
Differentiates between natural and synthetic gems or minerals based on their molecular composition, often used in gemology.
Analyzes pigments and materials in historical paintings or artifacts non-destructively to aid restoration and authentication.
Identification of water pollutants such as dyes, antibiotics and pesticides
