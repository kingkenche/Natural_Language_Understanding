Capacity Building Program on 23 December 2024
The IITJ-JJM Centre for Sustainable Drinking Water organized a Capacity-Building Program on December 23, 2024, with the theme of “Sustainability of Water Sources in Rural Areas” to equip participants with advanced skills and knowledge for addressing water-related challenges in rural areas. A total of  225 participants  registered for the program, representing different states and union territories across India.  Dr. Arpit Khandelwal , Assistant Professor IIT Jodhpur delivered a lecture on " Emerging Trends in Real-Time Water Quality Monitoring ".  Dr. Manoj Kumar Jindal  of Water Centre delivered lecture on “ Ensuring Water Security in Rural Regions: A Quantity & Quality Monitoring ”.
