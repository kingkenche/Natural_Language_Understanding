Our Programs | Technology Innovation and Start-up Center (IITJ-TISC) | IIT Jodhpur
TISC offers three flexible incubation pathways designed to support startups at different stages of development. Each program is tailored to your needs, from ideation through scaling.
Pre-Incubation Programme For: Individual innovators with early-stage ideas who need validation and business model development
Duration: 3–12 months
Facilitation Charges: ₹1,500 + 18% GST per year
Support Includes: Mentorship and guidance on idea validation
Access to TISC resources and facilities (limited lab access)
Capacity building and entrepreneurship training
Fellowship opportunities and grant linkages
Job listing support and community engagement
