Integrated Teaching Education Program (ITEP)
From Academic Year 2024-25, IIT Jodhpur is offering admission to ITEP, leading to BSc-BEd degree with specializations in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, with a focus on the Secondary stage. The first batch will have a total intake of 50 students with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics background in class XII with a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate or an equivalent grade from a recognised Board/University.
IIT Jodhpur will offer admission into its ITEP program based upon National Common Entrance Test (NCET) - 2024. Know more at  https://www.iitj.ac.in/itep/ .
