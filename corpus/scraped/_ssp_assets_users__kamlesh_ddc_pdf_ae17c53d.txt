How are books arranged in library?
Books  in  IIT  Jodhpur  Library  are  arranged  subject  wise  using  the  Dewey  Decimal
Classification  System  (22nd  Ed.).  In  this  system,  all  knowledge  is  represented  by  10
broad subject classes:
Computer science, information & general works
Philosophy & psychology
Technology (Applied sciences)
700  Arts & recreation
900  History & geography
The  Dewey  Decimal  system  is  a  hierarchical  system,  which  means,  within  each  broad
class, it develops progressively from the general to the specific.
Technology (Applied sciences)
Engineering and allied operations
Electrical, magnetic, optical, communications, computer engineering;
Internal storage (Main memory)
Semiconductor memory (bi-polar, MOS, thin-film memory etc.)
Each item in the Library has a unique call number and is arranged under that number on
the  shelves.  To  ensure  that  you  find  the  exact  item  you  want,  be  sure  to  copy  the
complete call number.
