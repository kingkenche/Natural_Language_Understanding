Speaker :- Dr. Kiran Meena
Title of the Talk :- Geometry using Riemannian maps
Date, Time and venue : 30th Sep, 2024 at 12:00PM - 1.00PM, @Meeting Room, Dept of
Abstract : In recent years, the theory of Riemannian maps has been analysed extensively.
In this talk, I will discuss the geometry of natural submanifolds arising out of Riemannian
maps. Towards this I will establish my (jointly published) results when the source manifold
admits Ricci soliton structure and also, I will describe the concept of Riemannian warped
product maps introduced in one of my (jointly published) papers. Finally, I will discuss the
Clairaut conditions for Riemannian maps and their important geometric implications.
About the speaker : Dr. Kiran Meena is currently working as Assistant Professor at Lady
Shri Ram College for Women, University of Delhi. She has obtained, Ph.D from Banaras
Hindu  University,  and  has  postdoctoral  experience  from  Harish-Chandra  Research
Institute. Her research area is Differential Geometry.
