Vibration Laboratory | Mechanical Engineering | IIT Jodhpur
Vibration and Control laboratory has been established with the objective of measuring the vibration characteristics and subsequent control for vibrating the machine or structures. This lab offers various experimental techniques and principles to study vibration analysis and control strategies. This lab is well equipped with various testing, measuring, and monitoring equipment for conducting the experiments and demonstrations for teaching and research purposes.
