Ashirvad Water Challenge Consultancy
A team from IIT Jodhpur (Dr. Akanksha Choudhary, Prof. Pradip Tewari and
Dr. Vikky Anand) has signed MoU with Nudge Institute as evaluation partner for
the Pan-India Ashirvad Water Challenge with Prize money 2.75 Crore. Twenty-five
(25) semi-finalists have been assessed and evaluated in the different domains
of water challenges.
