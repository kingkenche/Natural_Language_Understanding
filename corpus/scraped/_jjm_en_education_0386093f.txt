The Center for Sustainable Drinking Water is committed to advanced education and research. The center offers postgraduate (Master’s) and PhD programs, along with specialized coursework related to sustainable water solutions. Post-doctoral research is also actively conducted here, contributing to cutting-edge developments in the field. Additionally, Junior Research Fellows (JRF) and Senior Research Fellows (SRF) are engaged in diverse research projects, further strengthening the academic and research environment.
