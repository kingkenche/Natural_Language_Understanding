AI-driven Healthcare leverages artificial intelligence to revolutionize medical practices through predictive analytics, personalized treatment plans, and preventative care strategies. By integrating technologies like AIOT, multi-omics, and sensor systems, it enables precise tracking of health metrics and tailored interventions. This approach aims to enhance patient outcomes while advancing research in personalized and integrative medicine.
