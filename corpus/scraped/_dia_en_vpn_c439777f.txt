The IITJ VPN (Virtual private Network) with SSL client is a software application that is installed on a remote computer. The client makes a secure connection from the remote computer to your protected network through an unsecured network, such as the Internet. The VPN client uses SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) to secure the connection. User can see instructions and settings for connecting to IITJ VPN facility via below links.
