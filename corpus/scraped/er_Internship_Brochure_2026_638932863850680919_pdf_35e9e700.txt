KHOW TO REACH IIT JODHPUR Known as the “Blue City” and the “Gateway to Thar,”Jodhpur is a blend of modernity and tradition.Students can explore historic forts, vibrant markets, andcultural festivals while enjoying a safe and welcominglocal community, rich in culture.With growing industrial hubs, start-ups, and government-backed projects in Rajasthan, Jodhpur is slowlybecoming a hotspot for internships, live projects, andindustry exposure.IIT Jodhpur is located on NH-62, Karwar, about 24 kmfrom the city center, and is easily accessible by air, rail,and road. The Jodhpur Airport (28 km) has daily flightsfrom Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, and more.Jodhpur Junction Railway Station (25 km) connects thecity to major metros like Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, andChennai. Road links connect Jodhpur to Jaipur (330km), Udaipur (250 km), Ahmedabad (450 km), and Delhi(600 km).
