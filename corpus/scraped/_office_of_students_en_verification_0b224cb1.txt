Procedures for Document Verification
For the official verification of student documents, it is mandatory to follow the procedure through the link provided below.
Step 1: Register Request & Generate Request ID
Please click the link and complete the required actions; -
Agency Details:  Official organization name, contact person, registered email address, etc.
Student Details:  Full name, roll number, and specific documents to be verified.
After submission, a unique  Student Verification Request ID  will be generated. Please keep this Request ID safe for all future correspondence.
Step 2: Acceptance of Request
The office will review the submitted details and documents.
You will receive a notification once the office accepts the request.
Step 3: Payment of Verification Fee
The following charges are applicable as under through verification portal for verification of documents:
For Agencies in India
For Agencies outside India
Seeking Reply through email (₹)
Seeking Reply through Post/Courier (₹)
Seeking Reply through Email (US $)
Seeking Reply through Post/Courier (US $)
1500+GST = 1770
2000 GST = 2360
Once the request is accepted, the payment link will automatically activate.
Make the payment only through the same portal link.
Download the payment receipt after a successful transaction.
Step 4: Processing of Verification
The verification request will be processed within 7 working days after receipt of payment.
Any deficiency or clarification required will be communicated to the agency.
The final verification report will be issued after completion and can be downloaded from the portal itself.
