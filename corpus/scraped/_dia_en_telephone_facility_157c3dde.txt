IIT Jodhpur provides IP telephone to all Faculty, Group A officers, departments and various offices. Users may contact by dialing 0291 280 xxxx where “xxxx” is intercom number of concern section.
Institute Telephone Directory
The dialing procedure for accessing Intercom, local, STD & ISD facilities from your extension phone is as follows:
Within the campus dial four digit intercom number.
Call on Mobile: Dial 90 before 10 digit mobile number.
Local call in Jodhpur on landline: dial 9 and seven digits of landline number without area code.
Call anywhere in Rajasthan/India on landline : Dial 90 and STD code before landline number.
Call outside of India: Dial 00 before the destination number.
