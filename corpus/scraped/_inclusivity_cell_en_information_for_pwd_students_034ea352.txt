Information for PWD Students
Basic facilities for PWD Students.
All the hostels are well equipped with ramps, a Lift facility & separate washroom to cater to the needs of PWD Students.
Assistance is available for PWD students in hostels.
In general, PWD students are provided a room on the ground floor and can request assistance. The Hostel Caretaker or Security Guard provides support to them.
Accessible washrooms and common areas in the hostels for PWD Students.
Yes, the hostels have accessible washrooms and common areas designed to accommodate the needs of PWD students.
Safety measures for PWD students in hostels.
The hostels are equipped with safety features such as ramps, and handrails to ensure the safety of PWD students.
