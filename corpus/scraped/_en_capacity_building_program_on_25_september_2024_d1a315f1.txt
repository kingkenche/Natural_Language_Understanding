Capacity Building Program on 25 September 2024
Prof. Pradip K. Tewari , the distinguished JJM Professor Chair at IIT Jodhpur, emphasizing water sustainability's importance. A total of  256 participants  registered for the program, representing various States and Union Territories across India.  Prof. Arindam Malakar , University of Nebraska–Lincoln Nebraska Water Centre, USA gave a talk on ‘ A way to protect groundwater quality: Understanding reactive nitrogen transformation in the vadose zone ’. Prof. Farhat Naz , Associate Professor School of Liberal Arts (SOLA) talked about ‘ Community Engagement for Water Security and Water Sustainability ’.
