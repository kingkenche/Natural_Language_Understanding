International Dual Degree
SME, IIT Jodhpur has tie-ups with some of the leading business schools in the world and jointly offers dual degree programs. The students would successful complete their foundational courses in the 1st year at SME, IIT Jodhpur, and thereon would study specialized Master’s degree offerings in the 2nd Year at one of the partner B-School abroad. The students would go to earn an MBA/MBA-Technology degree from IIT Jodhpur and a specialized Master’s degree from the partner B-School.
The students can pursue dual degree options as listed below: DEGREE-1 (IIT JODHPUR) DEGREE-2 (PARTNER INSTITUTION) DURATION MBA-Technology MS - Digital Forensics and Cybersecurity (SUNY-Albany Business School-USA) (Details) 1+1 years MBA MS - Finance (Brandeis International Business School-USA) (Details) 1+1 years MBA-Technology MS - Business Analytics (Brandeis International Business School-USA) (Details) 1+1 years MBA MA - International Economics & Finance (Brandeis International Business School-USA) (Details) 1+1 years MBA Technology Master of Business Analytics and Intelligence (Case Western Reserve University) (Details) 1+1 years MBA Master of Finance (Case Western Reserve University) (Details) 1+1 years MBA Master of Supply Chain Management (Case Western Reserve University) (Details) 1+1 years
