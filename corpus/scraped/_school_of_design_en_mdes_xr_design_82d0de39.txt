MDes in XR Design
Course Plan The MDes in XR Design Program is meticulously designed to equip students with the essential skills and expertise required to thrive in the dynamic field of extended reality (XR) design. Over the course of four semesters, students will delve into a comprehensive curriculum that blends theoretical knowledge with hands-on experience, enabling them to become proficient XR designers capable of shaping the future of immersive technologies. First Semester focuses on building the core skills required by anyone entering the field of design. Second Semester offers introductory compulsory courses and electives in XR and closely associated fields, that will help students gain knowledge and build skills required for the program. In the Third Semester the students will build advanced skills in their area of study through further electives and start off their thesis project. In the Fourth Semester students will focus and complete their project work which they started in the third semester.
