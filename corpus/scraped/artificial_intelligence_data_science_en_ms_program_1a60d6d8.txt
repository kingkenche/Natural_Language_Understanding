MS by Program
MS Program IIT Jodhpur invites applications for admission to the M.S. (by Research) program offered by the Centre of Excellence on Brain Science and Applications (CBSA) in the School of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, IIT Jodhpur.
The MS by Research program at present is designed to offer degrees in the following areas:
MS by Research in Next Generation AI
Hosted by: Center for Brain Science and Applications
Co-ordinator: Dr. Dipanjan Roy
MS by Research in Computational Economics
Hosted by: Center for Mathematical and Computational Economics, SAIDE
Co-ordinator: Dr. Abhinaba Lahiri
MS by Research in Intelligent Infrastructure
Hosted by: Center of Intelligent Infrastructure
Co-ordinator: Dr. P. Ravi Prakash
MS by Research in Precision Health and Integrative Medicine
Hosted by: Center of Precision Health and Integrative Medicine
Co-ordinator: Prof. Mitali Mukerji
