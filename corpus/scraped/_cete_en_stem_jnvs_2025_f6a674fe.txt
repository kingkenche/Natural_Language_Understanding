STEM Teacher Training for JNVs 2025
The  STEM Teacher Training Program for PM Shri Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya  is being organized during  08 - 11 December 2025  at IIT Jodhpur, in association with Navodaya Leadership Institute, Udaipur. The need for STEM teacher training is vital to equip educators with skills to foster critical thinking and innovation in students, aligning with NEP 2020’s emphasis on experiential and interdisciplinary learning. The  Center for Education Technology (CET)  at IIT Jodhpur is happy to welcome teachers for an extensive Teacher Training Program that would focus on competency-based science education, technology-enhanced learning, experiential learning and holistic development of teachers and learners. Please visit  STEM Teacher Training  for more details.
