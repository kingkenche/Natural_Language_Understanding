Capacity Building Program on 21 December 2023
Prof. Pradip Kumar Tewari  delivered a lecture on "Water Sustainability" and discussed case studies to better understand the challenges and technical solutions.  Dr. Abhilasha Maheshwari  delivered a lecture on " Smart Water Infrastructure For Rural Areas ” “and  Dr. Shobhana Singh  presented “ Smart Water Distribution System – Opportunities and Challenges ”.  Dr. Saran Adhar  delivered talk on “ Sustainability of Water Resources under Changing and Anthropogenic Climate ”. One full session was dedicated to the experience sharing by the participants and to technical discussions.
