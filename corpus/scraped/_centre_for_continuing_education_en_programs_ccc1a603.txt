Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur invites applications for admission under the Quality Improvement Programme (QIP) 2026–27 for M.Tech. and PhD programmes.
The Quality Improvement Programme, launched by the Government of India, is implemented and monitored by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). The programme aims to enhance the academic and research capabilities of faculty members from AICTE-approved institutions by enabling them to pursue M.Tech. and Ph.D. programmes at premier institutes. Only sponsored teachers are eligible for admission to these programmes.
Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur is one of India's leading IITs, known for its strong focus on interdisciplinary research and innovation. The Institute hosts a vibrant academic community comprising 300+ faculty members and 4,000+ students, along with multiple academic departments and specialised research centres spanning engineering, science, management, and interdisciplinary domains. The Institute continues to expand its academic ecosystem with state-of-the-art laboratories, research facilities, and collaborative centres.
At IIT Jodhpur, we are committed to fostering excellence in research and higher education. Through the QIP programme, faculty members will have the opportunity to work in an advanced research environment, collaborate with leading academicians, and strengthen their teaching and research capabilities.
We encourage eligible faculty members to apply to join this initiative to strengthen technical education in India.
