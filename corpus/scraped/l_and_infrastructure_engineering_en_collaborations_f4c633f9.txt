Collaborations | Civil and Infrastructure Engineering | IIT Jodhpur
1. National Highway Authority of India (NHAI) signed MoU with IITJ on Friday, 24th October 2020 The MoU will facilitate the joint working of the two organizations in various domains including transportation engineering, structural engineering, geotechnical engineering, hydraulics engineering, infrastructure systems, earthquake engineering, urban and rural planning, and disaster management and planning. This MoU will also allow IIT Jodhpur's undergraduate and postgraduate students to undertake paid internships in any of the above-mentioned fields. This collaboration will help to bridge the industry-institution gap and develop technologies which can be adopted in practice. 2. The Department has collaboration with Jodhpur Development Authority (JDA), Jodhpur Nagar Nigam (JNN) , Jodhpur Traffic Police, Department of IT and Communications (DoIT&C) as part of the planned projects under Jodhpur City Knowledge and Innocvation Cluster (JCKIC).
