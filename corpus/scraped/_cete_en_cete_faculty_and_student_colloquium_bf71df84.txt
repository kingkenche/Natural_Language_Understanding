CETE Faculty and Student Colloquium
The CETE Colloquium is a weekly gathering of students and faculty members to promote intellectual exchange, collaboration, and the dissemination of knowledge within the field of education. It aims to nurture the growth of both students and faculty members while fostering a strong sense of community and dedication to educational research.
Every Wednesday, 5 PM
