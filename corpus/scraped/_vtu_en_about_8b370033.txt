Who We Are
The Office of Vocational Training & Upskilling (VTU), IIT Jodhpur, functions as a nodal office for short-term, vocational, skill-based and upskilling programmes offered by the Institute.
VTU is designed to:
Support national skilling initiatives
Enable industry-relevant workforce development
Provide structured vocational pathways alongside academic education
Deliver quality-assured training using IIT Jodhpur’s academic and infrastructural strengths
To become a nationally recognized centre for industry-ready vocational education and workforce upskilling, rooted in academic rigour and societal impact.
Deliver market-relevant, demand-driven skill programmes
Support Skill India, PMKVY, Mission Karmayogi and allied initiatives
Foster employability, entrepreneurship, and lifelong learning
Create sustainable skill pipelines through industry and government collaboration
National Alignment VTU programmes are aligned with:
Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY 4.0)
National Skill Qualification Framework (NSQF)
National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)
Government e-Marketplace (GeM) & GFR 2017
CCCD @ IITJ
