Scholarships | Office of Students | IIT Jodhpur
The Office of Students serves as a communication channel for various scholarship schemes launched by state and central government bodies. The Office administration intimates, motivates, guides and assists students in applying for any applicable scholarships/financial aid. The Office also performs correspondence between the Institute and Government agencies.
