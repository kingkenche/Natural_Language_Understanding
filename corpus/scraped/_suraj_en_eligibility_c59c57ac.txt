Eligibility | Summer Undergraduate Research at Jodhpur (SURAJ) | IIT Jodhpur
1.      Who Can Apply
Undergraduate students from NITs/IITs/IISERs, and should be currently enrolled in that institute as regular students.
Exceptional students from other prestigious institutions should be currently enrolled in that institution as regular students.
B.Tech./B.E. – in 3rd year
M.Sc. – in 1st year
Academic Requirements:  Minimum CGPA ≥ 7.0 / 10 (after 5th semester for B.Tech. students and after 1st semester for M.Sc. students.)
2.     Stipend Eligibility
An intern will be eligible for a stipend of Rs. 6000/- per month (for a maximum duration of 2 months) subject to the following conditions
Pursuing B.Tech./B.S. (Completed 5th Semester) or M.Sc . (Completed 1st Semester) at NITs (having MoU with IIT Jodhpur)
Minimum CGPA: 7.0/10 (At the end of 5th Semester for B.Tech./B.S. and 1st Semester for M.Sc .) A satisfactory grade from the project mentor at the end of the internship.
Even if a student does not meet the above criteria, they will be allowed to participate in the internship without receiving the stipend and faculty members may provide stipend through projects.
