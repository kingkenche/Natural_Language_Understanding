PHD ADMISSIONS FOR AY 2025-26 SEMESTER-I (JULY 2025)
SHORLISTED CANDIDATES FOR
ADMISSION TEST AND/OR INTERVIEW FOR PHD ADMISSION IN THE SCHOOL
OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE & DATA SCIENCE (SAIDE)
Important Instructions for the Shortlisted Candidates:
1.  The  written  test  and/or  interview  for  the  Ph.D.  admissions  (July  2025  session)  will  be
conducted  on  May  3  (Saturday)  and  May   8  (Thursday), 2025. The reporting time is 9.30
AM  on  the  respective  date.  Please  find  below  the  list  of  shortlisted  candidates  for  the
written test and/or interview.
2.  To  confirm  your  candidature  and  select  your  preferred  date, please fill out the following
Google Form by April 28 (Monday), 2025: https://forms.gle/P8ARY423SXxMWbyL6
3.  If the school decides to conduct a written test, the syllabus will be shared via email by April
29,  2025.  Notably,  the  candidates  listed  under  the  NET-JRF  or  INSPIRE  Fellowship
category  are  exempted  from  the  written  test  and  will  directly  appear  for  the  interview.
Foreign  candidates  will  be  contacted  by  the  SAIDE  office  for  verification  of  their
candidature and to schedule the interview.
4.  The  Part-time  Online  PhD  program  has  been  discontinued  starting  from  the  July  2025
admission  cycle.  As  per  the  institute  guidelines,  candidates  who  applied  under  this
category  may  now  be  considered  for  admission  under  the  External  PhD  category.
Accordingly,  all  eligible  candidates  from  the  Part-time  Online  category  have  been
included in the list of shortlisted candidates for External PhD positions.
5.  The  candidates  are  required  to  arrange  their  own  travel;  no  financial  support  will  be
provided by SAIDE, IIT Jodhpur.
