FROM THE DIRECTOR'S DESK
Professor Santanu Chaudhury
Director, IIT Jodhpur
It gives me immense joy to welcome you to the School of Management and Entrepreneurship (SME),
the new academic unit in the IIT Jodhpur system. SME aims to combine technology and management
to prepare students to explore a future of possibilities. SME is set to play a crucial role in delivering
cutting edge management and entrepreneurial education to existing and future professionals. With its
future  centric  curriculum,  exceptional  faculty,  and  strong  international  footprint,  SME  has  the  right
ingredients to achieve a leadership position among its peers with time to come.
SME has been conceptualized with a broader vision to evolve not only as a center of excellence for
management training and business education but also contribute to the overall academic vibrance of
IIT  Jodhpur.  To  this  end,  SME  will  also  be  offering  educational  programs  in  Management  and
Entrepreneurship and equip the students from other academic units on these critical skills that could
better prepare them for their specialized careers and engage in job creation.
Besides,  SME  is  also  poised  to  contribute  to  the  practice  outreach  of  IIT  Jodhpur  by  being  an
innovation  hub.  SME  will  contribute  to  the  community  through  cutting  edge  training  and
development programs to enable seasoned professionals across sectors to hone their managerial
skills and business acumen. I wish SME and all its stakeholders a purposeful and joyful journey
ahead  and  facilitate  all  its  endeavours.  I  invite  you  to  be  part  of  this  exciting  journey  and
participate in the success of SME at IIT Jodhpur.

FROM THE CHAIRPERSON's DESK
Professor Anuj Pal Kapoor
Chairperson- Corporate Relations and Placements
Committee, SME, IIT Jodhpur
School  of  management  and  entrepreneurship  (SME)  is  a  young
management  institute  formed  under  the  aegis  of  IIT  Jodhpur  in
2019.  The  MBA  and  Tech  MBA  programs  offered  at  SME  are
based  on  an  industry  4.0  curriculum,  which  not  only  provides
academic rigour to the students but also brings in the softer aspect
of  decision  making.  It  is  imperative  to  prepare  leaders  on  global
platforms to become holistic, well rounded human beings. Because
in  businesses,  values  are  ultimately  what  matters.  At  SME,  we
strive  to  imbibe  the  right  fundamental  values  based  on  the
integrity,  transparency,  role  of  business  in  society  as  part  of
The  School  of  Management  and  Entrepreneurship
(SME) at IIT Jodhpur was  founded  with its vision  of
research.The  curriculum,  at  SME  is  designed  in  a
unique  bifurcation  of  MBA  and  Tech  MBA  and  also
provides  the  opportunity  of  completing  the  degree
from  partnered  universities  globally.  The  school  is
also  a  pioneer  in  the  field  of  introducing  the
mandatory  Social  Sector  Attachment  course  to  be
completed  by  the  students  in  their  first  year  thereby
fabricating the leaders of tomorrow and instilling them
with foresight and humanistic values.
