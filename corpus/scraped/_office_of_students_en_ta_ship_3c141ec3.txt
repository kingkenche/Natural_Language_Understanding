Teaching Assistantship Teaching assistantship is granted to M.Tech. and Ph.D. students in return of their services in assisting the Faculty members in the conduct of ongoing courses. A consolidated amount of 12,400/- is provided to the M.Tech. students and 31,000/- and 35,000/- to the JRF and SRF Ph.D. students respectively, per month.
