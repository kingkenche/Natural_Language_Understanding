MBA COHORT 2024-26
The School of Management and Entrepreneurship, IIT Jodhpur proudly announces 100% Summer Placement
for the MBA Cohort of 2024–26,  marking it as the fifth consecutive year of this outstanding achievement. This
continued  success  reflects  the  unwavering  dedication  of  our  students,  the  tireless  efforts  of  our  faculties,
placement commitee members and the strong support from our industry partners.
This year’s placement season was marked by significant momentum, with 45+ recruiters participating in the
process — including 50% returning companies and an impressive addition of 20+ new corporate partners. The
season  saw  the  active  participation  of  industry  leaders  and  emerging  innovators  alike,  who  offered  diverse
and high-impact internship roles.
Among  the  prestigious  organizations  that  placed  their  trust  in  our  students  were:    EY,  Aditya  Birla  Group,
Reserve Bank of India, UBI, CBRE, Mediversal Healthcare, BOB, Apex Hospitals and many more.
The  season  witnessed  a  rise  not  only  in  the  number  of  offers  but  also  in  the  diversity  of  roles  spanning  IT
services,  consulting,  BFSI,  e-commerce,  manufacturing  etc  —  demonstrating  the  growing  recognition  of  our
program in the industry.

As  we  celebrate  this  success,  we  look  forward  to  strengthening  our  corporate  relationships  and  scaling  new
