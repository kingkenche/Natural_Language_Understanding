BET Surface Area & Pore Size Analyser
BET Surface Area and Pore size Analyser
MAKE: Quanta Chrome
MODEL: Autosorb iQ3
Fully automated analyser for surface area and pore size measurement.
Operate with any non-corrosive adsorbate and a wide variety of coolants. Dedicated helium input port and backfill gas input port.
High sensitivity is ensured by multiple pressure transducers, minimum void volume and accurate coolant level control.
It can also calculate the total pore volume of the sample.
Mode of use: Nitrogen at 77K
Sample required: 200 mg (minimum) (suggestion: please carry enough sample) in the form of solid/porous/ powder form.
Measures the surface area and porosity of catalysts, which directly affect their activity and efficiency in chemical reactions (e.g., in petrochemical or environmental applications).
Assesses the surface area of drug powders, influencing dissolution rate, bioavailability, and stability in tablet or capsule formulations.
Characterizes electrode materials like activated carbon or metal oxides, where high surface area enhances energy storage capacity and performance.
Samples are characterized in solid/porous/powder form with a minimum quantity of 200mg
