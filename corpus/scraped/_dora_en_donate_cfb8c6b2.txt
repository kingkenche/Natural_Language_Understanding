Give Back to Your Alma Mater
At IIT Jodhpur, every alumnus is an integral part of a legacy built on innovation, knowledge, and impact. Your journey here was more than just academics—it was about shaping your future, building lifelong connections, and becoming part of a community that continues to transform lives.
Today, you have the opportunity to make a meaningful difference by giving back to your alma mater. Your support—whether through donations, mentorship, or resources—will directly contribute to empowering current students, strengthening academic excellence, and fueling groundbreaking research.
When you give back, you are not only honoring the role IIT Jodhpur played in your life but also ensuring that future generations of students have the same opportunities to dream, innovate, and achieve. Every contribution, big or small, is a token of gratitude that strengthens the bond between you and your alma mater. Together, we can create new possibilities, nurture talent, and continue the tradition of excellence that defines IIT Jodhpur.
