Capacity Building Program on 23 September 2025
The IITJ-JJM Center for Sustainable Drinking Water, IIT Jodhpur, under the leadership of Prof. Pradip K. Tewari, organized a one-day Capacity Building Programme on  Water Source Sustainability for WASH in Rural Areas  on  23rd September 2025  via the SPM Niwas Digital Academy. With  300+ registrations from 25+ States/UTs , the event featured keynote talks by  Prof. Saugata Datta  (USA) on water sustainability in the Bengal Basin,  Prof. Dr. Khin-Ni-Ni Thein  (Myanmar) on community-driven WASH solutions, and  Dr. Kavita Verma  (IISc Bangalore) on groundwater recharge in semi-arid areas. The programme also included an interactive technical discussion, fostering cross-regional dialogue on innovative approaches to rural water security under the Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM).
