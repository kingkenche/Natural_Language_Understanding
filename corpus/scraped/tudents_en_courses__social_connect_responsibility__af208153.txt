Courses (Social Connect & Responsibility)
Courses on Social Connect and Performing Arts/ Sports The Institute is offering a course on Social Connect & Performing Arts/ Sports that aims at helping the students in breaking the isolation, building connect with their peers, with the society and with the environment and practicing responsible connect through sustainability practices. This in turn will help the students to be well versed in the social structure of the Institute, their objectives and issues. The Office helps in coordinating this course by working in close association with the faculty advisors and students who actually devise solutions to the problems faced by the local community. Another course being coordinated by the Office is on Performing Arts. In this course, the students are exposed to a wide range of activities like dance, theatre, music, painting, sketching, sculpture making etc, thereby enabling the students to hone their skills while being guided by experts from their respective fields.
