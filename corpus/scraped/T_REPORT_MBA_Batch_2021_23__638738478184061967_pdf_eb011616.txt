FINAL PLACEMENT HIGHLIGHTS
With  the  successful  completion  of  the  final  placement  season,  IIT  Jodhpur’s  School  of  Management  and
Entrepreneurship witnessed a commendable performance from students across all domains and sectors. The students
secured  offers  across  various  sectors  such  as  BFSI  (Banking,  Financial  Services,  and  Insurance),  Conglomerate,
Consulting,  cybersecurity,  FMCG,  Hospital  and  Healthcare,  Motor  Vehicle  Manufacturing,  and  IT/ITeS  (Information
Technology/Information  Technology-enabled  Services),  among  others.  Some  of  our  prominent  recruiters  include
American  Express,  Paytm,  Deloitte,  PWC,  EY,  Maxlife  Insurance,  Cognizant,  HCL,  Royal  Enfield,  Kottak  811,  Fluidech,
Mediversal, Zycus, Tata Technologies, Aditya Birla, IDBI Bank Ltd and to name a few.
Some of the roles offered by these recruiters were as follows: Product Manager, Technical Program Manager, Marketing
Operations,  Consultant,  AI  Analyst,  Financial  Advisory  Project  Manager,  Sales  and  Business  Enablement,  Operations
Manager,  Campus  Recruitment  Spl,  Sales  Incentive  Servicing  Process  Manager,  Cyber  Risk  &  Regulatory  -  SRC,
Management Trainee, etc.
The resounding triumph of SME IIT Jodhpur serves as an indubitable testament to its exceptional prowess in the realm
of  management  education.  With  utmost  gratitude,  SME  IIT  Jodhpur  extends  its  sincerest  thanks  to  all  the  esteemed
corporate partners for their invaluable collaboration throughout this academic year, and eagerly anticipates nurturing
and fortifying these alliances in the years that lie ahead.
