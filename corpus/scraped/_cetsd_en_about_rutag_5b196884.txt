About RuTAG | Center for Emerging Technologies for Sustainable Development | IIT Jodhpur
Rural Technology Action Group (RuTAG) Office of the Principal Scientific Adviser (PSA) to the Government of India conceptualized a mission in 2003-2004 called Rural Technology Action Group (RuTAG). The aim of RuTAG was to provide a science and technology intervention and development of appropriate technologies for rural areas.
