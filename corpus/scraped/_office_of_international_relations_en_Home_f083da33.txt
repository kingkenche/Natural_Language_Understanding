The Office of International Relations at the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur is dedicated to fostering global partnerships and enhancing the institute’s international presence. Our mission is to build and maintain strategic relationships with universities, research institutions, and industry leaders worldwide to promote academic collaboration and cultural exchange. We manage a range of activities, including international partnerships, student and faculty exchange programs, and global research collaborations. Our team works to create opportunities for IIT Jodhpur’s community to engage with the global academic and professional networks, facilitating joint research projects, internships, and study abroad experiences. The Office of International Relations is instrumental in supporting international students and faculty, providing them with the resources and assistance needed to integrate seamlessly into the IIT Jodhpur community. We also coordinate international events, conferences, and seminars that highlight the institute’s global engagement and expertise. Our office is committed to enhancing the institute’s global reputation and expanding its international footprint. By leveraging our network of global partners, we aim to create enriching opportunities that contribute to the academic and professional growth of our students and faculty. We continually seek to innovate and expand our international programs to align with the evolving global landscape and the strategic goals of IIT Jodhpur.
