Welcome to the IIT Jodhpur Technology Innovation and Start-up Center (IITJ-TISC)
TISC (Technology Innovation and Start-Up Centre) is a premier incubator dedicated to empowering entrepreneurs and fostering innovation. We provide personalized support, including technical assistance with advanced prototyping labs, business development guidance, and administrative help to navigate legal and financial landscapes. Our offerings encompass seed funding, connections with a robust network of investors, and skill development opportunities through workshops and seminars. With state-of-the-art workspaces, collaborations with IIT Jodhpur faculty, and mentorship from seasoned entrepreneurs and industry experts, TISC transforms visionary concepts into successful ventures, driving impactful solutions for a better future.
Welcome to TISC IIT Jodhpur — where ideas evolve into impact and innovation meets purpose.
As the Chief Executive Officer of TISC IIT Jodhpur , it is my privilege to lead this BIRAC BioNEST Centre and work alongside passionate innovators who aspire to build technologies that matter. At TISC, we don’t just support startups — we partner in your journey to transform research into real-world solutions.
Our Mission & Vision
Our mission is simple yet powerful: to ignite, nurture, and scale high-impact technology ventures by seamlessly connecting academia, industry, investors, and government enablers.
Our vision is ambitious: to establish TISC IIT Jodhpur as one of Rajasthan’s leading innovation hubs, while accelerating research commercialization that delivers national relevance and global impact.
Strengthening the Regional Innovation Ecosystem
TISC is proud to be at the forefront of Jodhpur’s growing startup renaissance. Located within IIT Jodhpur, we offer startups access to world-class infrastructure, advanced prototyping facilities, and Centers of Excellence (CoEs) in areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Biotechnology, Renewable Energy, and other emerging domains.
Beyond infrastructure, startups benefit from deep technical mentorship from IIT faculty, strong industry and investor networks, and structured access to government schemes and funding programs designed to accelerate growth.
Your Innovation Journey Starts Here
Whether you are at the ideation stage, developing an MVP, or scaling a high-growth startup, TISC IIT Jodhpur offers customized incubation support, funding linkages, mentorship, and market access tailored to your needs.
We invite you to leverage this ecosystem advantage and join us in building solutions that shape the future.
Let’s innovate, build, and scale — together.
