TEM Sample Preparation Units

1. ULTRASONIC DISC CUTTER
Quickly cut TEM discs from hard, brittle materials such as semiconductors, ceramics, and geological materials
Disc size: 3mm in diameter for surface analysis, 4x5 mm and disc for cross-sectional analysis
Cutting drive: Piezoelectric crystal
Slurry Material: Boron carbide
2: DISK PUNCH
3mm disc from metals, alloys, and all ductile materials for TEM
3: DISC GRINDER
For Pre-thinning and polishing of TEM grids up to 100um
Uniformity Accuracy: 5um
4: DIAMOND WIRE SAW
Make: β tech
Wire Specifications: 300um
Grain size: 40um
Wire Consumed in one run: 10 meters
Wire Speed range: 0.1-3.6m/sec
Wire tension- 2kg
Cutting Force: Gravity drawn- +-300gm
Max. sample weight: 2500 Gm
Sample Size: Max- 50x50x50 mm or 50mm diameter
Min cutting thickness: 200um (depending on sample)
Sample Type: Glass, Ceramic, Abrasive, Plastic, Rubber, Wood, Ferrous, Nonferrous, Biological-Bone,Tooth, Tissue (after plastination)
5: DIMPLER GRINDER
Model: Dimpler Grinder II-657
Capable of flat thinning as well as dimpling 3 mm diameter TEM samples down to 10 ums
Utilizes both the large wheel and flat wheel to preserve a large transparent area after processing
Produces a final thickness of < 3 μm in dimpled specimens
6: ION BEAM MILLING SYSTEM
Model: PIPS II System-695
Voltage range: 0.5- 8kV
Two ion guns
Milling angle: +10° to -10°.
Ion current density: 10 mA/cm2
Current range : 0-100 microamps.
Temperature range: -150° to 30°C
