Greetings from the School of Design at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Jodhpur. We are excited to announce that our placement drive for the pioneering batch of M.Des in XR Design students (2023-25) has officially commenced. Our students bring expertise in a wide range of areas, including XR Design, UI/UX Design, Experience Design, XR Prototyping, User Research, Game Design, Environment and Space Design, Character Design, Design Thinking, 3D Modeling, Animation, XR Development, and much more. They have presented their work at top conferences like India HCI and Futuring Design Education and have gained hands-on experience through internships with leading companies such as Maersk Training, Godrej, TCS Interactive, NCRTC, and MadVR. Please refer to the Placement Brochure of School of Design for more information. We look forward to the opportunity to collaborate with your esteemed organization.
