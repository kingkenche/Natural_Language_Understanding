About Us | Office of Stores & Purchase | IIT Jodhpur
The Office of Stores & Purchase facilitates the following:
Purchase of equipment & stores;
Maintenance of Asset Register; Stock Verification;
Maintenance of Control Store; Maintenance of lists of stockists and dealers for various items of regular purchase;
Liaison with State Purchase Cell and Customs;
Processing of write-off & disposal of unserviceable stores/equipment;
Advising in the framing of rules for Purchase and Maintenance of Stock Registers, Stock Verification, write-off, etc.;
Advising Departments, Centers for Technologies and other academic service facilities with respect to stores & purchase; and
Advertisements on request of Offices of the Institute. Sustainable Procurement IIT Jodhpur is adopting Sustainable procurement for their needs of goods, services, works and utilities to achieve “value for money on a whole life basis”. The Life Cycle Cost (LCC) represents the total cost of its ownership and includes all the costs likely to be incurred during the life of the items to acquire it, operate it, support it, and finally dispose of it. This benefits not only IIT Jodhpur but also society, the economy and avoiding damage to the environment. Link to sustainable public procurement [SPP] policy - click here
