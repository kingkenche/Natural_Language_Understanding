Robotics and Intelligent Machines
Robotics and Intelligent Machines focus on creating autonomous systems that integrate advanced AI and sensory technologies to perform complex tasks in dynamic environments. By combining machine learning, computer vision, and human-robot interaction, these systems can adapt and learn from their surroundings, enhancing efficiency and functionality across various applications. This field aims to revolutionize industries such as healthcare, manufacturing, and transportation, paving the way for smarter, more responsive machines.
