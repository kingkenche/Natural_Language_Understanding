Artificial Intelligence for Information and War Gaming Technologies (AIWT)
Today’s multi-agent Warfare and Wargaming Technology involves the application of Artificial Intelligence in Information gathering, analysis, and decision-making. Ensuring realistic, unbiased simulations requires vast, high-quality datasets—often limited or classified in military contexts. AI algorithms can be Utilized to model the unpredictability of human-machine behavior in complex conflict scenarios. Cybersecurity and Information warfare threats loom large, as AI systems are vulnerable to data poisoning and manipulation. Additionally, integrating AI with legacy systems and ensuring interoperability across platforms adds complexity.
