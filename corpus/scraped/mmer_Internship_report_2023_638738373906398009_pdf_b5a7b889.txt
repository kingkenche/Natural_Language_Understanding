The  School  of  Management  and  Entrepreneurship,  IIT  Jodhpur  is  delighted  to  announce  the  successful
completion  of  100  percent  summer  placements  for  the  MBA  cohort  of  2022-24.  This  marks  the  third
consecutive  year  that  our  institution  has  achieved  this  remarkable  milestone,  which  is  a  testament  to  the
unwavering commitment of our faculty and students toward excellence in education.
With  the  addition  of  20+  new  campus  recruitment  partners,  this  placement  season  witnessed  more  than  60
companies conducting the recruitment process and offering attractive opportunities to our students.
American  Express,  Bosch,  Maxlife  Insurance,  Inditex(Zara),  Bank  of  Baroda,  Royal  Enfield,  Mediversal  Multi
Super Specialty Hospital, Zycus, TimesPro, Sutherland, Reserve Bank of India (RBI), and many more esteemed
organizations joined hands with us for the summer placements. This season, we also witnessed a remarkable
improvement in the number of corporate partners and the stipend offered to the students.
We would like to extend our deepest gratitude to all our campus partners, faculty, staff, alumni, and students
for  contributing  extensively  to  making  this  summer  placement  a  grand  success.  We  would  like  to  appreciate
your  continued  confidence  in  us  throughout  this  zealous  endeavor,  and  we  look  forward  to  amplifying  the
bond we have forged.

School of management and entrepreneurship (SME) is a young management institute formed
under  the  aegis  of  IIT  Jodhpur  in  2019.  The  MBA  and  Tech  MBA  programs  offered  at  SME  are
based on an industry 4.0 curriculum, which not only provides academic rigour to the students
but  also  brings  in  the  softer  aspect  of  decision  making.  It  is  imperative  to  prepare  leaders  on
global platforms to become holistic, well rounded human beings. Because in businesses, values
are ultimately what matters. At SME, we strive to imbibe the right fundamental values based on
the integrity, transparency, role of business in society as part of management education.
