Inductively Coupled Plasma-Mass Spectrometer (ICP-MS)
Inductively Coupled Plasma-mass Spectrometer (ICP-MS)
Mass Range Up to 260 amu
Interface Ni/Cu cones (standard), optional Pt cones for high matrix tolerance
Detector: Dual-stage discrete dynode electron multiplier with 10⁹ dynamic range
Sensitivity > 1,000,000 cps/ppm for ⁸⁹Y
Background < 1 cps (with shield torch)
Oxide Ratio (CeO⁺/Ce⁺) < < 0.5%
Doubly Charged Ratio (Ba²⁺/Ba⁺) < 1.5%
Isotope Ratio Precision < 0.1% RSD (typical)
Dynamic Range Up to 10⁹
Collision/Reaction Cell ORS4 (Octopole Reaction System) with helium collision mode
Integrated Software Agilent ICP-MS MassHunter with IntelliQuant
Environmental Analysis: Detection of trace metals in water, soil, and air samples
Clinical and Biomedical Research: Measurement of essential and toxic elements in biological fluids
Food and Beverage Testing: Analysis of contaminants like heavy metals in food products to ensure safety and regulatory compliance.
Geological and Mining Exploration: Determination of elemental composition in rocks, minerals, and ores for resource identification and evaluation.
Plant Science and Agriculture: Detection of metal accumulation in different parts of plants and agricultural products.
