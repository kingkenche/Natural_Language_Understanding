Rashtriya Avishkar Abhiyan (RAA) Initiative
OPEN HOUSE FOR SCHOOL STUDENTS
21 November 2015
The salient objectives of the event at IIT Jodhpur are to: Enable students in getting motivated and engaged in Science, Mathematics, and Technology;  Provide an exposure to students of national needs and current science.
Plan of Activities
The proposed plan of activities for that day is mentoring of 100 students from Classes VIII and IX in the neighbouring schools of the city through:
Public Lectures;  Laboratory Visits.
