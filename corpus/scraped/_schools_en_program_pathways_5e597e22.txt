Program Pathways | School of Management & Entrepreneurship | IIT Jodhpur
The program has two pathways: Mode 1: Student completes two semester coursework at IIT Jodhpur and joins SUNY Albany from the third semester. Following this pathway, the student will acquire two degrees – MBA in FinTech & Cybersecurity from IIT Jodhpur, and MS in Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics from SUNY Albany. Mode 2: Student completes all four semesters at IIT Jodhpur (first two semesters common with path.) Following this pathway, the student will acquire an MBA in FinTech & Cybersecurity from IIT Jodhpur.
