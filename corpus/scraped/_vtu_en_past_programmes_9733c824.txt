Rashtriya Karmayogi Facilitator Workshop – Phase II
Rashtriya Karmayogi Facilitator Workshop – Phase II-
(Mission Karmayogi | Capacity Building Commission)
Program Type: Government Capacity Building
National Mission: Mission Karmayogi – Capacity Building Commission
Program Phase: Phase II (Facilitator Development)
Duration: 3 Days (Intensive Residential / Campus-based)
Mode: Offline (Facilitated Workshop)
Certification: Rashtriya Karmayogi Facilitator Certification
The Rashtriya Karmayogi Facilitator Workshop (Phase II) is a structured, high-impact 3-day capacity-building program designed to develop certified facilitators capable of delivering the Rashtriya Karmayogi Program under Mission Karmayogi.
The program focused on behavioral transformation, facilitation excellence, nation-building mindset, and experiential learning, enabling participants to serve as change agents within government systems. This program marked the Office of VTU, IITJ formal entry into Mission Karmayogi-aligned public service capacity building.
● Government officials
● Identified Rashtriya Karmayogi Facilitators
● Public service trainers and master trainers
● Officers nominated under Mission Karmayogi
