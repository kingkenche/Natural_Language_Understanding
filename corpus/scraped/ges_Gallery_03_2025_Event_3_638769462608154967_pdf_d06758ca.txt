Title of Event- RAISE-2024 Recent Advances in Sustainable Energy
Date of start of event- 26 December 2024
Details  of  event-  This  workshop  will  delve  into  the  latest  breakthroughs  and
innovations  in  the  field  of  sustainable  energy.  We  will  explore  a  wide  range  of
topics,  including  renewable  energy  sources,  energy  storage  technologies,  energy
efficiency  strategies,  and  the  integration  of  sustainable  energy  systems  into  the
global energy landscape.
